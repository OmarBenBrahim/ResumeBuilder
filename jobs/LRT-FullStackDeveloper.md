About us:

At LRT Innovation Labs, we’re driving the future of electric mobility by combining the precision of German engineering with local Tunisian talent. As the first EV charging infrastructure manufacturer in Tunisia, our mission is to develop innovative, sustainable technologies that meet global standards.

We are looking for an experienced Full Stack Developer to join our cloud team. In this role, you will build user-facing components and multiple applications for various users, including car owners, business owners, home users, and solar panel owners. The position involves both front-end and back-end tasks, with a focus on personal preferences and skills in consultation with the team and project requirements.

Exigences de l'emploi

Develop and maintain user-facing components and applications using internal APIs.

Collaborate with the team to design and implement solutions for different user groups.

Optimize applications for performance and scalability.
