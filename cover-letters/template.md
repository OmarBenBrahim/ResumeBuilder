# Cover Letter Template

Use this template as a starting point for cover letters.

---

## Template

```
[Your Name]
[Your Email] | [Your Phone]
[Your LinkedIn] | [Your Portfolio]

[Date]

[Hiring Manager Name or "Hiring Team"]
[Company Name]
[Company Address]

Dear [Hiring Manager/Hiring Team],

[OPENING - 2-3 sentences]
Express enthusiasm for the specific role and company. Mention how you found the position and why it excites you.

Example: "I am excited to apply for the [Position] role at [Company]. With [X years] of experience in [relevant field] and a passion for [company's focus area], I am confident I can contribute to your team's success."

[BODY PARAGRAPH 1 - 3-4 sentences]
Highlight your most relevant experience. Connect your background directly to the job requirements. Use specific examples and metrics.

Example: "In my current role as a Full Stack Developer, I've built AI-powered applications that reduced operational workload by 40-60% for clients. My expertise in [specific technologies from job description] has enabled me to deliver scalable solutions that directly impact business outcomes."

[BODY PARAGRAPH 2 - 3-4 sentences]
Provide another relevant example or expand on your skills. Show how you can solve their specific challenges.

Example: "I've successfully delivered [specific achievement relevant to job]. This experience has prepared me to [specific contribution you can make to their team]."

[CLOSING - 2-3 sentences]
Reiterate enthusiasm, mention next steps, thank them for consideration.

Example: "I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my skills and experience align with [Company]'s goals. Thank you for considering my application, and I look forward to the possibility of contributing to your team."

Sincerely,
[Your Name]
```

---

## Key Points to Remember

1. **Customize for each application** - Reference specific job requirements
2. **Keep it concise** - One page maximum, 3-4 paragraphs
3. **Show enthusiasm** - Explain why this company specifically
4. **Include metrics** - Quantify your achievements
5. **Mirror their language** - Use keywords from the job description
6. **Proofread carefully** - No typos or errors

---

## Phrases to Use

### Opening

- "I am excited to apply for..."
- "With [X years] of experience in..."
- "Your commitment to [company value] resonates with..."

### Body

- "In my current role, I have..."
- "I successfully delivered..."
- "My expertise in [skill] enabled..."
- "This resulted in [quantified outcome]..."

### Closing

- "I would welcome the opportunity to discuss..."
- "I am eager to bring my skills to..."
- "Thank you for considering my application..."
